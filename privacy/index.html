Privacy Policy — Honk Out

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ATANY (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the mobile application Honk Out (also referred to as BusJam) (the “App”).

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

ATANY

Website: https://atanyco.com

Contact for privacy questions: atany@atanyco.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you do not need to provide

You can play the core game without creating an account and without providing your name, email address, or other contact details to us.

2.2 Information stored on your device

To run the App, we store game-related data on your device, such as:

  • Game progress (for example, levels completed and related scores)
  • In-app currency balances and unlock status
  • Preferences and settings you choose in the App

This information stays on your device unless you back up, restore, or transfer your device using Apple’s system features. We do not operate a separate account system that uploads this progress to our own servers.

2.3 Purchases (StoreKit / Apple)

If you make an in-app purchase, the purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. Apple may collect and process payment and purchase information under Apple’s own privacy policy. We receive purchase status needed to deliver the items or features you bought (for example, whether a purchase succeeded and what was unlocked). We do not receive your full payment card number.

2.4 Information Apple or the system may provide

Depending on your device settings and Apple’s services, the App or Apple may process limited technical information needed to operate the App and App Store features, such as device type, OS version, and App Store / TestFlight-related diagnostics that Apple makes available to developers.

2.5 What we do not intentionally collect

As of the date above, the App is not designed to require:

  • User accounts or social login
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location for core play
  • Advertising identifiers for third-party ads

If we add analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud sync, or similar features later, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features collect personal information.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and maintain the App and your game progress on your device
  • Process and deliver in-app purchases
  • Fix bugs, improve performance and gameplay, and support TestFlight / App Store distribution
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms
  • Respond if you contact us

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only as follows:

  • **Apple** — to process purchases and operate App Store / TestFlight services
  • **Service providers** — only if we later use vendors who help us run the App (for example hosting a support inbox); they may process information only to provide those services
  • **Legal / safety** — if required by law, or to protect ATANY, users, or others
  • **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this policy or a successor policy

5. Retention and deletion

On-device game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear App data using your device controls. Deleting the App removes the App’s local data from that device (subject to any device backups you keep).

Purchase records tied to your Apple ID are retained by Apple under Apple’s policies. If you contact us about a privacy request, we will help where we can and direct you to Apple for purchase-related records we do not control.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own marketing. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through a channel we control (for example an email to us), contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights for information we control, email atany@atanyco.com.

You can also:

  • Avoid in-app purchases
  • Delete the App to remove local App data from your device
  • Use Apple ID / App Store settings for purchase and subscription controls

8. International users

We may process information in the countries where we or our service providers operate. If you use the App from outside those locations, your information may be transferred across borders as needed to provide the App and respond to you.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
